# VERIFIED
> UNESCO: Global Youth Hackathon 2026

### VERIFIED is a game inspired by everyday life in Mexico, designed to simulate the daily interactions of a family unit constantly exposed to misinformation. Your goal is to develop the ability to detect risky information, identify its underlying purpose, and share this knowledge with those around you.

Given the growing threat posed by harmful political and economic interests present on social media, VERIFIED is a system grounded in prebunking theory that builds resistance against disinformation by exposing players to manipulation techniques in controlled doses, while situating the experience within Mexico's cultural and linguistic reality. It challenges users to classify content according to Wardle's typology (2017) and to recognize the motivations behind it (profit, propaganda, provocation, or belief). By incorporating humor and familiarity as core strategies, VERIFIED not only educates but also builds socially situated resistance, overcoming the limitations of generic interventions.

## Support and technical specifications

The application is based on React + Vite. It features 26 NPCs, with custom dialogues via Gemini 3.6 Flash that operates exclusively within the family group chat in the WhatsApp application.

It offers the player a wide range of endings, publications, mechanisms, and scenarios depending on their decisions throughout the gameplay.
